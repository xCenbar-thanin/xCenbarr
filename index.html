កិរិយាសព្ទ to be (ជា / គឺ / នៅ)

FormI am · you are · he/she/it is · we/they are

«to be» ប្រើប្រាប់ឈ្មោះ អាយុ មុខរបរ សញ្ជាតិ អារម្មណ៍ និងទីតាំង។ ភាសាអង់គ្លេសតម្រូវឲ្យមានកិរិយាសព្ទនេះជានិច្ច ទោះភាសាខ្មែរអាចលុបបានក៏ដោយ។

ទម្រង់ខ្លី៖ I'm, you're, he's, she's, it's, we're, they're។ បដិសេធ៖ am not, isn't, aren't។ សំណួរ៖ ដាក់ be នៅមុខប្រធាន (Are you ready?)។

Formenjoy/finish/avoid/mind + -ing · want/decide/hope/plan + to + verb

កិរិយាសព្ទខ្លះតម្រូវឲ្យមាន -ing និងខ្លះតម្រូវឲ្យមាន to ។ ត្រូវរៀនជាក្រុម មិនអាចទាយបានទេ។

ក្រោយបុព្វបទ (in, at, about, of) ប្រើ -ing ជានិច្ច៖ I'm interested in learning English។

  • She finished cleaning at noon.នាងសម្អាតចប់នៅថ្ងៃត្រង់។
  • I decided to leave.ខ្ញុំសម្រេចចិត្តចាកចេញ។
  • He avoids eating sugar.គាត់ចៀសវាងញ៉ាំស្ករ។
  • Thank you for helping me.អរគុណដែលបានជួយខ្ញុំ។
Common mistake

I enjoy to dance. → I enjoy dancing.

Present perfect vs past simple

Present perfect ប្រៀបធៀប Past simple

Formhave/has + V3 (ទាក់ទងបច្ចុប្បន្ន) · V2 (ពេលវេលាចប់ជាក់លាក់)

ប្រើ past simple ពេលមានពេលវេលាជាក់លាក់ ឬគេដឹងថាពេលណា (yesterday, in 2019, last week, when...)។

ប្រើ present perfect ពេលពេលវេលាមិនសំខាន់ ឬនៅមិនទាន់ចប់ (ever, never, already, yet, for, since, so far, this week)។

for = រយៈពេល (for three years)។ since = ចំណុចចាប់ផ្តើម (since 2020)។

  • I have lived here for ten years.ខ្ញុំរស់នៅទីនេះបានដប់ឆ្នាំហើយ។
  • I lived in Battambang in 2015.ខ្ញុំរស់នៅបាត់ដំបងក្នុងឆ្នាំ ២០១៥។
  • She has worked here since March.នាងធ្វើការនៅទីនេះតាំងពីខែមិនា។
  • Have you seen the news today?តើអ្នកបានមើលព័ត៌មានថ្ងៃនេះទេ?
Common mistake

I am living here since 2010. → I have lived here since 2010.

ជាមួយ since/for ប្រើ present perfect មិនមែន present continuous ទេ។

Try it
  1. He ___ (work) here for five years. has worked
  2. When ___ you ___ (arrive)? did / arrive

Present perfect continuous

Present perfect continuous

Formhave/has + been + verb-ing

ប្រើសម្រាប់សកម្មភាពដែលចាប់ផ្តើមក្នុងអតីតកាល ហើយនៅបន្តមកដល់ឥឡូវ ឬទើបតែឈប់ ដោយផ្តោតលើរយៈពេល ឬដំណើរការ។

ពាក្យសញ្ញា៖ for, since, all day, lately, recently, how long...?

  • I've been learning English for two years.ខ្ញុំកំពុងរៀនអង់គ្លេសបានពីរឆ្នាំហើយ។
  • She has been waiting since noon.នាងរង់ចាំតាំងពីថ្ងៃត្រង់។
  • It has been raining all day.ភ្លៀងពេញមួយថ្ងៃ។
  • How long have you been working here?តើអ្នកធ្វើការនៅទីនេះបានយូរប៉ុណ្ណាហើយ?
Common mistake

I have been knowing him. → I have known him.

កិរិយាសព្ទស្ថានភាពមិនប្រើទម្រង់ continuous ទេ។

Try it
  1. They ___ (play) football for two hours. have been playing
  2. How long ___ you ___ here? (live) have / been living

Past perfect

អតីតកាលបរិបូរណ៍

Formhad + past participle (V3)

ប្រើសម្រាប់សកម្មភាពដែលកើតឡើង មុនសកម្មភាពមួយទៀតក្នុងអតីតកាល។ សកម្មភាពមុន = past perfect។ សកម្មភាពក្រោយ = past simple។

ពាក្យសញ្ញា៖ before, after, by the time, already, when។

  • When I arrived, the film had started.ពេលខ្ញុំទៅដល់ ភាពយន្តបានចាប់ផ្តើមហើយ។
  • She had already eaten.នាងបានញ៉ាំរួចហើយ។
  • I hadn't seen him before that day.ខ្ញុំមិនធ្លាប់ឃើញគាត់មុនថ្ងៃនោះទេ។
  • Had they left when you called?តើពួកគេបានចាកចេញហើយឬនៅ ពេលអ្នកទូរស័ព្ទ?
Common mistake

When I came, he already left. → When I came, he had already left.

សកម្មភាពមុនត្រូវតែជា past perfect។

Try it
  1. After she (finish) ___, she went home. had finished
  2. He ___ never flown before 2020. had

Passive voice (present and past)

ប្រយោគអកម្ម (បច្ចុប្បន្ន និងអតីត)

Formbe + past participle (+ by agent)

ប្រើពេលសកម្មភាព ឬលទ្ធផលសំខាន់ជាងអ្នកធ្វើ ឬមិនដឹងថាអ្នកណាធ្វើ។ ប្តូរកិរិយាសព្ទ be តាមកាល (is made, was made, will be made, has been made)។

  • English is spoken worldwide.ភាសាអង់គ្លេសត្រូវបានគេនិយាយទូទាំងពិភពលោក។
  • The bridge was built in 1960.ស្ពានត្រូវបានសាងសង់ក្នុងឆ្នាំ ១៩៦០។
  • My bike has been stolen.កង់របស់ខ្ញុំត្រូវបានគេលួច។
  • The report will be finished tomorrow.របាយការណ៍នឹងបញ្ចប់ថ្ងៃស្អែក។
Common mistake

The house built in 1990. → The house was built in 1990.

Try it
  1. Rice ___ (grow) in many countries. is grown
  2. The letter ___ (send) yesterday. was sent

Second conditional

ល័ក្ខខ័ណ្ឌប្រភេទទី២

FormIf + past simple, would + base verb

ប្រើសម្រាប់ស្ថានភាពស្រមៃ ឬមិនពិតនៅបច្ចុប្បន្ន/អនាគត។ ប្រើ were ជាមួយគ្រប់ប្រធាន (If I were you...) ក្នុងភាសាទ្រង់ទ្រាយ។

  • If I won the lottery, I would travel.បើខ្ញុំត្រូវឆ្នោត ខ្ញុំនឹងទៅដើរលេងបរទេស។
  • If I were you, I would apologize.បើខ្ញុំជាអ្នក ខ្ញុំនឹងសុំទោស។
  • She wouldn't go if it were dangerous.នាងមិនទៅទេ បើវាគ្រោះថ្នាក់។
  • What would you do if you were rich?តើអ្នកនឹងធ្វើអ្វី បើអ្នកមានទ្រព្យ?
Common mistake

If I would have money, I would buy it. → If I had money, I would buy it.

  1. If I (have) ___ a car, I would drive. had
  2. If he ___ (be) here, he would help. were

Reported speech (basic)

ពាក្យនិយាយតប់ (មូលដ្ឋាន)

Formsaid (that) + subject + verb (back-shift)

ពេលនិយាយតប់ ត្រូវរុញកាលថយក្រោយមួយជំហាន៖ present → past, will → would, can → could, have done → had done។

ប្តូរសព្វនាម និងពេលវេលា៖ I → he/she, now → then, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day។

  • "I am tired." → She said she was tired.នាងថានាងហត់។
  • "I will call you." → He said he would call me.គាត់ថាគាត់នឹងទូរស័ព្ទមកខ្ញុំ។
  • "I can swim." → She said she could swim.នាងថានាងចេះហែលទឹក។
  • He asked if I was ready.គាត់សួរថាតើខ្ញុំរួចរាល់ឬនៅ។
Common mistake

He said me he was late. → He told me he was late.

tell ត្រូវមានអ្នកទទួល (told me)។ say មិនត្រូវទេ (said to me)។

Try it
  1. "I work here." → She said she ___ there. worked
  2. "I will go." → He said he ___ go. would

Modals of deduction

Modals ទាយដោយហេតុផល

Formmust / might / may / could / can't + base verb

ប្រើទាយអំពីបច្ចុប្បន្នដោយផ្អែកលើភស្តុតាង។ ទំនុកចិត្តពីខ្ពស់ទៅទាប៖ must (ប្រាកដណាស់) → might/may/could (ប្រហែល) → can't (មិនអាចទៅរួច)។

  • He must be at work.គាត់ប្រាកដជានៅធ្វើការ។
  • She might be sick.នាងប្រហែលឈឺ។
  • That can't be true.រឿងនោះមិនអាចពិតបានទេ។
  • It could be a mistake.វាអាចជាកំហុសមួយ។
Common mistake

It mustn't be true. → It can't be true.

ដើម្បីបញ្ជាក់ថាមិនអាចទៅរួច ប្រើ can't មិនមែន mustn't ទេ។

Try it
  1. The lights are on. He ___ be at home. must
  2. She just left. She ___ be far away. can't

Used to vs be/get used to

Used to ប្រៀបធៀប be/get used to

Formused to + verb · be/get used to + verb-ing

used to + verb = ទម្លាប់ក្នុងអតីតកាល។ be used to + -ing = ស៊ាំនឹង (ឥឡូវ)។ get used to + -ing = កំពុងចាប់ស៊ាំ។

  • I used to get up late.ខ្ញុំធ្លាប់ក្រោកយឺត។
  • I am used to getting up early.ខ្ញុំស៊ាំនឹងការក្រោកព្រលឹម។
  • She is getting used to living abroad.នាងកំពុងចាប់ស៊ាំនឹងការរស់នៅបរទេស។
  • He isn't used to the cold.គាត់មិនស៊ាំនឹងអាកាសត្រជាក់ទេ។
Common mistake

I'm used to get up early. → I'm used to getting up early.

ក្រោយ to នៅទីនេះជាបុព្វបទ ត្រូវប្រើ -ing។

Try it
  1. I'm not used to ___ spicy food. (eat) eating
  2. We ___ to live in Phnom Penh. used

Verbs + gerund / infinitive (meaning change)

កិរិយាសព្ទ + gerund / infinitive (ន័យប្រែ)

Formstop/remember/forget/try + -ing or to

កិរិយាសព្ទខ្លះប្តូរអត្ថន័យតាមទម្រង់ដែលតាមពីក្រោយ។ stop doing = ឈប់ធ្វើ។ stop to do = ឈប់ដើម្បីធ្វើ។

remember/forget doing = ចាំ/ភ្លេចអ្វីដែលបានធ្វើហើយ។ remember/forget to do = ចាំ/ភ្លេចត្រូវធ្វើ។ try doing = សាកធ្វើ។ try to do = ខំធ្វើ។

FormWere + subject... · Had + subject + V3... · Should + subject + verb...

Had I would known... → Had I known...

  • All I did was ask a question.អ្វីដែលខ្ញុំធ្វើគឺសួរសំណួរតែប៉ុណ្ណោះ។

What I need it is time. → What I need is time.

Modals កម្រិតខ្ពស់

I will call you but I forgot. → I was going to call you but I forgot.

Modal nuance: shall, dare, need, ought to

ព័ត៌មានលម្អិតរបស់ modals

Formshall · dare · need (modal) · ought to

shall (British) = ការផ្តល់ជូន ឬស្នើ (Shall I help you?)។ ក្នុងច្បាប់ shall = ការតម្រូវ។ dare/need ជា modal ក្នុងសំណួរ និងអវិជ្ជមាន (Need I come? He daren't speak)។

ought to = ចាំបាច់ខាងសីលធម៌ (ស្រដៀងនឹង should) តែទ្រង់ទ្រាយជាងបន្តិច។ ប្រើជាមួយ have + V3 ដើម្បីបញ្ជាក់សោកស្តាយ (You ought to have asked)។

  • Shall I open the window?តើខ្ញុំបើកបង្អួចទេ?
  • You needn't worry.អ្នកមិនចាំបាច់បារម្ភទេ។
  • How dare you speak to me like that!ហ៊ានយ៉ាងណាអ្នកនិយាយមកខ្ញុំបែបនោះ!
  • You ought to have told me.អ្នកគួរតែប្រាប់ខ្ញុំ។
Common mistake

He needs not to come. → He needn't come. / He doesn't need to come.

ជា modal ប្រើ needn't ឬជា verb ធម្មតាប្រើ doesn't need to។

Try it
  1. ___ I carry your bag? (Shall/Will) Shall
  2. You ___ have asked first. (ought) ought to

Aspect and nuance: simple vs continuous vs perfect

ភាពខុសគ្នាស្រាលៗនៃទម្រង់

Formsimple (fact) · continuous (in progress) · perfect (relevance) · perfect continuous (duration)

simple = ការពិតរួចសព្វគ្រប់។ continuous = កំពុងដំណើរការ ជាបណ្តោះអាសន្ន ឬមិនទាន់ចប់។ perfect = ទំនាក់ទំនងនឹងពេលនិយាយ។ perfect continuous = ដំណើរការមានរយៈពេល។

continuous ជាមួយ always បង្ហាញការរំខាន (He is always complaining)។ ប្រើ continuous ដើម្បីបន្ទន់ការបញ្ជា (I was wondering if you could help)។

  • I live in Phnom Penh. (permanent)ខ្ញុំរស់នៅភ្នំពេញ (ជាអចិន្ត្រៃយ៍)។
  • I'm living in Phnom Penh. (temporary)ខ្ញុំកំពុងរស់នៅភ្នំពេញ (ជាបណ្តោះអាសន្ន)។
  • He is always complaining.គាត់តែងតែត្អូញត្អែរ (គួរឲ្យរំខាន)។
  • I was wondering if you could help me.ខ្ញុំកំពុងគិតថាតើអ្នកអាចជួយខ្ញុំបានទេ (សុភាព)។
Common mistake

I am wanting to ask you. → I want to ask you. / I was wondering if...

want ជា stative។ ចង់សុភាព ប្រើ I was wondering។

Try it
  1. "He ___ (talk) too loudly!" (annoyance, always) is always talking
  2. Which is more polite? "I wonder" or "I was wondering"? I was wondering

Yet there are encouraging counter-examples, where recordings, schools and digital tools have helped revive languages once thought to be moribund. These cases suggest that decline is not inevitable, though revival demands sustained commitment from the speakers themselves.
